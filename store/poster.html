Nephele Wisp
Browser companion for Nephele Workshop

Built for artists who
refuse to be replaced.

Wisp fills the upload form on a platform you're already logged into with the post your desktop app prepared (image, title, caption, topic) — then stops at "draft ready" so you can review and click publish yourself.
